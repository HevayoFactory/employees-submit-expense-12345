# Expense Claims

## Problem Statement

Employees claim expenses, managers approve them and finance moves approved amounts into payroll. Without one shared place, claims get lost, approvals are slow to chase, and finance re-keys approved amounts into payroll by hand.

## Solution

A web app where employees submit expense claims, managers approve or reject them, and finance exports approved claims to payroll as a downloadable file.

## Actors

- Employee — submits and tracks their own expense claims.
- Manager — reviews and decides on claims from their team.
- Finance — sees approved claims and exports them to payroll.
